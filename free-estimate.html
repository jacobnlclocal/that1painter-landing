Knoxville Painters

House painters in Knoxville for every surface, inside and out

If you searched for painters near you, this is what we do and how we work. That 1 Painter of Knoxville is a locally run painting company: Lucas and Nick manage every project personally, crews are employees rather than day labor, and every job gets one fixed price in writing after a free walkthrough.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, accent walls, and whole-home repaints. Single rooms from $1,450; most multi-room projects $3,400 to $6,500.

Exterior painting

Siding, trim, soffits, shutters, and doors with wash, scrape, caulk, prime, and two coats. Most full repaints $4,800 to $13,500.

Cabinet painting

Kitchen and bathroom cabinets sprayed to a factory-smooth finish. Refinish instead of replacing.

Deck staining

Clean, sand, and stain or seal decks, porches, and pergolas so the wood survives Tennessee summers.

Fence painting and staining

Privacy fences, picket fences, and rails, painted or stained. Deck and fence staining runs $1,700 to $6,300 on recent jobs.

Drywall and wood repair

Nail holes, cracks, and minor drywall damage inside; rotted fascia and trim outside. Repaired before paint and itemized on the quote.
