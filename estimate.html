FAQs

Before you book

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The on-site walkthrough and the written price are free, and there's no obligation to book the job.

What happens after I fill out the form?

Our booking calendar opens so you can choose a day and time for the walkthrough. If you'd rather not pick a time online, just close it: Lucas or Nick will call you back within an hour during business hours (Mon to Fri 8am to 6pm, Sat 9am to 2pm ET).

How is the price set?

We walk the project with you, measure, and factor in prep, surfaces, and paint. Then you get one fixed, itemized price in writing. No hourly billing and no "it ran over" at the end.

Which areas do you serve?

Knoxville and all of Knox County, plus Farragut, Maryville, Alcoa, Powell, Karns, Hardin Valley, Oak Ridge, and Lenoir City. Call (865) 348-3036 if you're not sure we cover your address.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. That 1 Painter of Knoxville is licensed and insured in Tennessee, and every project comes with a written 3-year workmanship warranty.

Can you paint the outside of my house this late in the year?

Usually through early November. We don't paint below 50°F overnight or with rain in the forecast, so we schedule around the weather. Interior work runs all winter.
